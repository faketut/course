## Cross-check added 12 Nov 2026 — Nov 11 close does not exist; Nov 10 curve leans A
- Nov 11 2026 was Veterans Day: the U.S. bond market was closed and
  Treasury has published no Nov 11 par curve (latest published, read
  Nov 12 ~07:15 ET, is Nov 10). There is NO Nov 11 close cash price
  to fetch — the open item above should read "Nov 10 close", the last
  trading day. The CME Nov 11 12:10 CT snapshot is therefore Nov 10
  market data carried over, not Nov 11 trading.
- Independent cross-check from the Treasury Nov 10 par curve
  (verified live Nov 12: 5y 4.81%, 7y 4.89%, 10y 4.98%): interpolating
  to the note's ~6.75y maturity gives ~4.88%. Repricing the 3.875%
  note at 4.88–4.90% gives clean ~94.14–94.25 — matching CME's
  reported cash yield 4.9023% / derived 94.1427 (Scenario A), NOT the
  ~4.97% / ~93.71 implied by CME's -0.3539 net basis (Scenario B).
  A 9bp cheapness to the interpolated curve would be unusually large
  for this note (off-the-run cheapness is normally a few bp), so the
  curve evidence leans Scenario A — but it is a cross-check, not a
  settlement: the actual Nov 10 close cash price (TreasuryDirect
  end-of-day prices / WSJ quotes) is still the one number that
  settles A vs B.
- Futures-price wrinkle, flagged for the re-run: the model used the
