## Caveats (carry into agent design)
1. **Top-5, not top-1.** Worked answers split across chunks.
2. **Module 8 reading is still missing** (23pp scanned). Solutions
   cover strategies, but the reading's taxonomy is absent. OCR it
   before finalising Scenario #4, or state the gap.
3. **Retrieval ≠ calculation.** The RAG finds the right formula and
   worked example; the arithmetic for TDI (100 steel contracts,
   138 HDD contracts, 750t offsets) must be done in a calculator
   and checked by a human, not trusted to LLM arithmetic.
4. **External layer is separate and dated.** CME specs, Ontario EPS,
   carbon prices are not in this index. Tag them with source + date
   and re-verify on Nov 2; never let the agent cite a course chunk
   for a live market fact.
