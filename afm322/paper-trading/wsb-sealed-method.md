# WSB Agent — SEALED method (specific trade revealed Nov 2, after Jack locks)

## What is sealed, and what is NOT in this file
- This file deliberately contains NO specific ticker, strike or premium.
  A specific pick cannot honestly exist yet: it must come from the IBKR paper
  option chain on Nov 2, and inventing one on Nov 1 would be a fabricated trade.
- What is sealed is the METHOD the WSB agent will use on Nov 2, so the pick
  cannot be retrofitted to Jack's pick or to the market outcome.

## WSB method (fixed before Jack locks)
1. Universe: liquid November options in the IBKR paper account, expiry on or
   before Nov 30, closable by Nov 30.
2. Style: a directional, leveraged, short-dated play of the kind a retail
   momentum trader would post about — NOT a hedged, defined-risk textbook
   spread unless the chain makes that the clearest momentum expression.
   The agent must state its thesis in one sentence, in its own voice, and
   must NOT copy Jack's thesis.
3. Risk disclosure, computed from actual Nov 2 quotes before the trade is
   logged: net debit/credit, max profit, max loss, break-even(s) incl. costs,
   margin, and the price move needed to break even. If max loss is uncapped,
   say so in capitals — do not soften it.
4. Sentiment inputs (Reddit / social) are tagged UNTRUSTED in the log and are
   never a substitute for the chain quote or the payoff math.
5. Reveal rule: the specific pick is written to wsb-pick-nov2.md ONLY after
   Jack's pick sheet is locked (date/time recorded). The reveal file must
   quote the chain at the time of the pick, with a screenshot reference.

## Integrity note for the Scenario #4 document
- If Jack's pick is locked after the WSB reveal, or if either pick is changed
  after seeing the other's, the document must say so. A voided competition
  reported honestly is worth more than a clean-looking one that is not true.
